# Management and Disposal of Computer Equipment for Seniors: Technological Challenges and Solutions

**Academic Extension Project — FMU (Module III - Information Technology)**[cite: 28]

## 📌 Project Overview
This project addresses digital inclusion for senior citizens and sustainable management of electronic waste (e-waste). It highlights the main barriers faced by the elderly in adopting new technologies and proposes hands-on community workshops and personalized mentorship to promote digital autonomy and reduce social isolation[cite: 32, 36, 41].

## 🎯 Alignment with UN Sustainable Development Goals (SDGs)
- **SDG 4:** Quality Education[cite: 30]
- **SDG 10:** Reduced Inequalities[cite: 30]
- **SDG 12:** Responsible Consumption and Production[cite: 30]

## 💡 Proposed Solution & Methodology
- **Action Plan:** Interactive workshops focusing on basic device handling, internet safety, and fraud prevention[cite: 36, 39, 41].
- **E-Waste Management:** Community guidance on proper recycling and disposal of obsolete computational hardware[cite: 31, 39, 40].
- **Mentorship:** Peer-to-peer and youth volunteer support for continuous learning[cite: 36, 39, 41].
